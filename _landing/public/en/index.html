Three calculations others round off

These aren't details. The wrong compounding method skews your mortgage payment every single month, and one contribution too many costs 1% per month in penalty. The screens below come from the app.

Calculation 01

Your contribution room, anchored to the CRA

You enter the room shown in CRA My Account once, with its reference year. Fin:Track then projects every contribution and every withdrawal — including the rule that catches everyone: a TFSA withdrawal only restores your room on January 1 of the following year.

Calculation 02

The mortgage, with semi-annual compounding

In Canada, mortgage interest compounds twice a year, not twelve times. Apps built in the United States apply monthly compounding and get your payment wrong. Fin:Track uses the equivalent periodic rate, separates principal from interest, and counts the payments you have left.

Calculation 03

Credit lines, at the daily rate

A Canadian line of credit accrues interest every day on the balance you have drawn. Fin:Track shows the daily rate to four decimal places, estimates the real monthly cost, tracks your utilization, and handles all three minimum payment types instead of assuming one.

Your data stays where it belongs

A bank statement says where you live, where you eat, who you pay and when you were sick. Fin:Track is built so that information doesn't leave your device unless you ask it to.

Local by default

Your data lives in the app, protected by the system's encryption. No sign-up is required to get started.

Sync stays yours

Syncing across your devices runs through your own private iCloud account, not through our servers.

The bank connection is optional

It runs through a Canadian provider, it turns on only if you want it, and the app stays complete without it.
